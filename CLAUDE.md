# London Simulator

A UK driving and roleplay game for Roblox, set in London, inspired by Birmingham Vehicle Simulator: realistic cars, UK roads, jobs (taxi, bus, police, ambulance), wanted stars and arrests. The roadmap is in README.md.

## Who you're working with

The owner is new to Roblox Studio and coding, works on a Mac, and is in the UK. Explain things in plain words, give click-by-click steps, and show results (screenshots, what to press) rather than theory. They care most about the game looking as real as Birmingham Vehicle Simulator.

## Layout

The repo follows Rojo's layout (`default.project.json`), so every file maps to one script in Studio:

| Folder | In Studio |
| --- | --- |
| `src/shared` | ReplicatedStorage → Shared |
| `src/server` | ServerScriptService → Server |
| `src/client` | StarterPlayer → StarterPlayerScripts → Client |

`.server.luau` is a Script, `.client.luau` a LocalScript, any other `.luau` a ModuleScript; the name is the file name without the ending.

The game copies Birmingham Vehicle Simulator's flow and look; `docs/BVS-NOTES.md` has what we saw when we played it (menus, HUD, scale, roads) and how London maps onto it.

- `Main.server.luau` starts everything: lighting, player data, the map (Cambridge or London, `Config.Map`), traffic lights, cars, the garage, and a placeholder paycheque.
- `PlayerData` saves cash, owned vehicles and paint picks in a DataStore, and mirrors them as player attributes (`Cash`, `Owned_<id>`, `Paint_<id>`) for the UI. A failed load never overwrites a real save.
- `shared/Vehicles` lists every vehicle (made-up names, team, price, speed, body style, livery). `CarBuilder` uses the detailed body in `ServerStorage.VehicleBodies.<vehicle id>` when there is one, bolted onto our own invisible chassis (wheel hubs that spin and steer, seats where the model had them, lamps tagged with a `LightRole` attribute), and otherwise builds a stand-in from parts. Bodies come from 3D models run through `tools/prepare_vehicle_bodies.luau` in the Command Bar: it scales them to the map, strips every script, joint and sound, deletes blocked logo images, swaps real names in text, and finds the seats, wheels, lamps and paint. Bodies live only in the place file, never in this repo. The current ones are free Swat Motors Company stand-ins; the owner plans to buy realistic cars to replace them. **Our own drive system (18 Oct 2026, `src/client/RaycastCar.luau`):** after A-Chassis kept feeling twitchy next to BVS, every CarBuilder car (the 140, 340 and the rest) now rides on raycast springs with our own tyre forces, run by Driving on the driver's device through client-made VectorForces at each wheel hub plus a drag force and a stability Torque (the TyreContact balls stop colliding while driven). Springs 1.9 Hz / 0.45 damping, 1.2-stud ride length, 0.7 droop, anti-roll 0.6. Tyre: Pacejka-style curve (B 10, C 1.45 front / 1.52 rear), one grip budget per tyre shared between cornering and drive/brake, grip and brakes given as accelerations because Roblox gravity is ~5.6x real at our 0.28 m/stud scale. Stability: slide yaw capped at 1.15 x grip / speed + 0.2 rad/s, spin guard damps rotation past 30° slip, slide traction control past 20°. Measured on the pad: full-left/full-right swap changes direction in 0.15 s, handbrake flick at 50 mph kicks the back out, holds a ~40° drift, countersteer catches it in 0.4 s. Steering lock is 45° (`Config.SteeringLockDegrees`) held in full up to 15 mph, then fading, but full lock never asks less than the tightest grip-limited turn plus 4° (wheelbase x grip / speed²), so yanking it at speed can overpower the tyres. Full-lock U-turn widths (car centre, scratchpad `uturn.luau`): 26 studs at 6 mph, 28 at 10, 29 at 14, 34 at 18. Added 18 Oct 2026 after Lewis asked for real-life slides ("you need to fight it", "braking in a bend should kick the back out", "the back should jump about"): weight transfer, because Roblox's 5.6x gravity hides it (braking or lifting moves up to 35% of axle load forward; cornering moves load to the outside, 0.55 front / 0.7 rear, with tyre load sensitivity 0.3, so the back lets go first at the limit), ABS at 85% of a tyre's grip with a 70/30 brake split, a rear grip curve that drops to ~75% (C 1.6) so a regripping rear snaps, road-surface grip noise (±22%) on sliding rear tyres, and countersteer lock that doesn't unwind itself (0.6 rad/s), so holding it too long swaps the slide. Stability eased to SLIDE_YAW 1.25 / rate 8 and spin guard from 40°. Pad tests (scratchpad `feel.luau`, `slide3.luau`, `yank.luau`): braking in a 45 mph bend tightens the line with ~10° rear slip; full lock and throttle at 40 mph kicks the back out after ~0.4 s, countersteer catches in ~1 s with a snap the other way; handbrake flick at 45 slides to ~60° and takes ~1.3 s to catch; no countersteer spins. Wheel spin is wrapped and NaN-guarded (a tumble once left three wheels frozen), and rays that hit walls (normal under 0.3 of the car's up) don't count as road. Kit houses: pieces smaller than 1.2 studs or 400 cubic studs are non-solid. Lawns and yards are `TownKit.detail`. Kit unions and MeshParts use Box collision (set on the templates in Studio). Physics memory went from 2.9 GB to 0.6 GB.
    - Don't build the map in edit mode over and over: every build and clear goes into Studio's undo history (5 GB here). If you must, run `game:GetService("ChangeHistoryService"):ResetWaypoints()` afterwards, and restart Studio when memory gets high.
    - Parked cars (2 per 0.1 s) and pedestrians (1 per 0.25 s) are made gradually. The minimap hides everything outside its window. Thin details don't cast shadows, and low buildings have no back window grids.
    - Measure with a RenderStepped frame-time logger (frames over 50/100 ms) on a scripted drive (the `TestThrottle` attribute) and standing still, plus `Stats:GetTotalMemoryUsageMb()`. After the fix: 60 fps, no frames over 50 ms, 7 GB in Studio.

2. **`INSTALL.luau`**: one block pasted into Studio's Command Bar (Window → Command Bar) that creates or replaces every script. Regenerate it with `python3 tools/build_installer.py` after any change to `src/`.
3. **`starter.project.json`**: `rojo build starter.project.json -o LondonSimulator.rbxlx` builds a fresh place with the scripts, ground, spawn and Future lighting. Use it only for a brand-new place, because it doesn't contain the owner's map.

Playtesting through screen control: after typing in the Command Bar, click the game view twice before pressing keys, or Studio keeps the keyboard. Hold Space or F for about 0.3 s, since a tap is too short to register.

## Checks before committing

- Type-check in strict mode with luau-lsp and Roblox's type definitions: `rojo sourcemap default.project.json -o sourcemap.json`, then `luau-lsp analyze --definitions=<globalTypes.d.luau> --sourcemap=sourcemap.json --base-luaurc=<file containing {"languageMode": "strict"}> src/`. Definitions: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JohnnyMorganz/luau-lsp/main/scripts/globalTypes.d.luau
- With Studio MCP, also playtest and read the Output for errors.

## Rules

- **No real brands in the published game.** No car makers, logos, shop names, "Metropolitan Police" or "NHS". Use made-up names (the reference game calls its BMW-like car "Bayron"). Bought models with real badges get renamed and re-textured.
- **Check every script in bought or free models** for backdoors (`require` of an asset ID, `loadstring`, `getfenv`, HTTP calls, obfuscated text) before it goes in the game.
- **Bought 3D models (CGTrader etc.) mustn't be redistributed.** Never upload them to the Creator Store as public, never commit their files to this repo, and keep the place uncopyable.
- Code comments are short and plain, written for a beginner reading them in Studio. UK spelling in anything players see.
