# Handling reference, 18 Oct 2026 (watched live)

Lewis drove a yellow 2017 M140i ("BST M140") in a UK night-city car game he wants our driving to feel like.
What it does that ours doesn't:

- **Real physics car.** The HUD is the A-Chassis 6 style: analogue rev counter (x1000 rpm), km/h speedo, gear,
  boost gauge (about 20 psi on boost), TCS / ABS warning lights that flash when they step in, a Sunroof button.
  A-Chassis is the constraint-based car kit most realistic Roblox car games use: real springs and dampers, wheels
  as physical cylinders with tyre friction, engine torque through a gearbox and diff to the wheels.
- **Weight you can see.** The body squats under acceleration, rolls in corners and dips under braking, from the
  actual suspension rather than an animation.
- **Drifts.** On the throttle in 3rd-4th at 60-100 km/h the back steps out and it holds a long slide, with the
  traction-control light flickering. It can spin round to face the other way.
- **Camera follows where the car's going**, not locked behind it: in a slide you see the car side-on, and it
  pulls back a little at speed.
- **8-speed auto**, reached 191 km/h in 8th; about 70 km/h in 3rd.
- Brake light and LED rear lights work properly; front plate, rear yellow UK plate.
